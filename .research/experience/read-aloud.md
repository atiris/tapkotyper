# Tapko reads aloud

## What
- A setting "Tapko speaks" (off, instructions, instructions + words). Tapko reads the
  story lines, new-key intro cards, tips and break texts aloud; in the second mode it also
  says each target word when it appears and names the key ("left index finger, F").
- Uses the voices built into the computer, so it works offline; if no voice for the
  language exists, the setting explains it and stays off.
- Follows the existing sound button (quiet / off) and has its own speed slider.

## Why
- Typing.com launched Read Aloud in June 2026 with three levels (basic guidance, guidance +
  words, fully guided typing with finger cues) and different narrators per age group
  (https://www.typing.com/whats-new/1780786868/meet-read-aloud-lifelike-voices-for-every-lesson,
  https://support.typing.com/en/articles/13785921).
- TypingClub reads "all activity directions and lesson items" aloud and teachers turn it
  on for students who need it
  (https://freetech4teach.teachermade.com/?p=7551,
  https://www.wssb.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2021-08/AccessibleTypingClub.docx).
- Programs for dyslexic children (Nessy Fingers) are praised by parents of dyslexic and
  dysgraphic 11-year-olds (https://www.nessy.com/us/product/nessy-fingers/?pp=1); younger
  or weaker readers (8-9 years) skip text cards they cannot read quickly.
- Dance Mat's voice was hard for some children to follow
  (https://www.commonsensemedia.org/website-reviews/dance-mat-typing) - a choice of voice
  and speed avoids that.

## Fit
- No network, no new assets; complements the dyslexia fonts already offered.
- Risk: Slovak system voices are missing on some computers and sound robotic on others;
  the feature must be optional and honest about it.

## Priority
P2, effort M.
