# Accessibility

**Status:** new. **Prio:** P2.

## What
- Font choice: a readable default, with options such as Lexend, Atkinson Hyperlegible, OpenDyslexic. Large text size setting, wide letter spacing for target words.
- Colorblind-safe finger colors; every color also has a label or pattern.
- Reduce motion switch (calms parallax, shakes, moving targets); words sit still long enough to read.
- Sound: master mute, separate music and effects; everything works with sound off (visual cues for all audio feedback).
- Uncluttered screen: few moving objects, one target in focus.
- Optional spoken letter/word (browser speech) for pre-readers and dyslexic learners.
- Keyboard-only start to finish; high contrast mode; works for one-handed or slower typists via no-timer mode.
- Slovak and English UI text in simple words.

## Why
- Dyslexia guidance: text plus audio plus on-screen keyboard picture, uncluttered, low distraction from moving objects. [src: https://www.monmouthshire.gov.uk/touchtyping]
- Keybr recommended for dyslexic users for being simple. [src: https://dyslexiahelp.umich.edu/latest/keybrcom-simple-online-game-help-improve-typing-skills/]
- OpenDyslexic does not suit everyone; Lexend and Atkinson Hyperlegible suggested as alternatives. [src: search result, https://www.abdn.ac.uk/toolkit/productivity/opendyslexic/]
- Dance Mat: voice in British English hard for ELL students; giggly distractions. [src]
- No colorblind-specific typing game guidance found. [src gap]
