# Accuracy-First Feedback

**Status:** extends spec (spec has "gentle encouragement on mistakes" and a speed-driven rocket speedometer). **Prio:** P1.

## What
- Rewards (stars, Tapko items) come from clean, steady typing, not raw speed.
- Wrong key: target does not move or break; Tapko shows the right key. No lives, no game over, no red X screens.
- Speed is shown as personal progress ("faster than last week"), never as a rank against other kids.
- Rocket speedometer (Phase 2) should reflect rhythm and steadiness; slow-but-correct must still succeed. Consider a "cruise" speed floor so slow typists are not stuck.
- Results screen: accuracy first, speed second, one thing to try next.
- No public leaderboards, no races.

## Why
- Teachers say racing makes students chase speed over accuracy. [src]
- Kids complain when speed is prioritized; parents call scoring rigid and pacing discouraging. [src]
- Competition invites cheating and bans. [src]
- Reviewers liked a relaxed typing game over "type or die". [src]

## Risk
- Timed super-obstacles (tsunami, mole) can cause stress. Offer a "no timers" parent switch; timed events should slow down, never fail.
