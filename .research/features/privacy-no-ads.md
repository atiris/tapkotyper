# Privacy, No Ads, No Accounts

**Status:** extends spec (client-side only, localStorage). **Prio:** P1.

## What
- Plain-language promise on the start screen and in the parent area: no ads, no accounts, no analytics, no data leaves the device, no third-party scripts or fonts loaded from CDNs.
- Local profiles only; export/import JSON is the only "sync". Warn that clearing browser data deletes profiles; offer a reminder to export.
- Instead of teacher class codes: a printable progress card or a small exported file the teacher can collect. Optional later: shared-device mode for classrooms (profiles on one machine).
- No chat, no online leaderboards, no external links in the child view.
- Guest mode leaves no stored data.

## Why
- Ads are the top complaint: parents report "excessive ads" and free versions show "distracting banner ads". [src]
- Some typing sites carry a privacy warning from a review organisation. [src]
- Typing sites collect names, emails and cookies and allow third-party ad cookies; class rosters and SSO add burden for schools. [src]
- Unmoderated chat is flagged as a risk for young kids (opinion source). [src]
- Our selling point against free-but-ad-heavy tools.
