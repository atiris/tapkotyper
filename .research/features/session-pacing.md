# Session Pacing and Healthy Play

**Status:** extends spec ("zero addiction loops", "child-safe psychology pacing"). **Prio:** P1.

## What
- Default session target 10-15 minutes; parent can change. At the limit Tapko suggests a stretch and the game saves a calm stopping point (end of a small scene, not mid-obstacle).
- Break reminder with posture and wrist stretch after about 15-20 minutes.
- Progress can be finished in short sessions; no daily streaks, no "you lost your streak", no expiring rewards, no random loot boxes.
- Rewards are fixed and predictable (stars to accessories), not variable.
- Parent can set a daily soft cap and see play time. Cap is a gentle "see you tomorrow", not a lock.
- Suggest regular short practice (daily, 10-20 min) in the parent text.

## Why
- Advice: start with 5-10 min, break after about 20 min for primary age, move or stretch. [src: EANI, https://send.eani.org.uk/sites/default/files/2025-02/Touch%20Typing.docx]
- Teachers/parents suggest 15-20 min daily; 5-10 min few times a week still works; once a week is not enough. [src: https://community.typesy.com/t/do-you-have-some-tips-in-doing-typing-lessons-for-us-homeschool-parents/4379 (vendor forum, treat with care)]
- Competitive and score-chasing elements stress some learners. [src: Common Sense Media TypingClub parent reviews]
- Gamification addiction concerns: [bg] streaks, variable rewards and social comparison are the usual patterns to avoid.
