# The bubble: put a thing in a bubble with a word

Date: 2026-11-04. A brainstorm: main ideas, not a design yet.

## Idea (P2)
- A typed word wraps a thing in a bubble, or pops a bubble that holds a thing. One small action that
  can be used in many games.

## Two ways
- **Float away:** litter lies on the ground, the word puts it in a bubble and it floats away. The meadow
  gets clean piece by piece.
- **Pop and fall:** things float past in bubbles in a stream of words, the word pops the right bubble
  and the thing falls, for example onto a conveyor belt, into a basket or a nest.

## Why
- It is calm and clear: each word does one thing you can see.
- It fits the waiting-words games (things wait on the ground) and the stream games (things pass by).
- No lives: a bubble that floats past just comes again later.
