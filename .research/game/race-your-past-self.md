# Race your past self

## What
- In replayable levels and rescue missions, an optional "shadow Tapko" shows how the child
  played the same kind of level some time ago (for example last week): a faint second
  rocket, egg or stepping-stone path moving at that old rhythm.
- At the end Tapko compares only with the child's own past: "Your rhythm is smoother than
  two weeks ago." Accuracy stays the star score.
- Off by default; turned on by the child for a level.

## Why
- Competition is the reason children love typing races: kids enjoy practicing typing with
  their friends, and races engage kids more than traditional single-player typing trainers.
- Races are also blamed because students focus on speed not accuracy, and for chat and
  ads; racing yourself keeps the thrill without strangers.
- Showing growth against oneself is the healthy version of the speed trend the app
  already shows in numbers.

## Fit
- No other people, no leaderboard, nothing lost; but it adds a bit of speed focus. Keep it
  optional, use rhythm (not WPM) and never show it during the first world.
- Needs small recordings of earlier runs per level kind in local storage.
