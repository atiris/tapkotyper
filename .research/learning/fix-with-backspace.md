# Fix it yourself (Backspace)

## What
- An optional mode for children past the first worlds (switch in the parent area, off by
  default): a wrong key is written into the word in a soft color, and the child removes
  it with **Backspace** before going on.
- Backspace gets its own short intro card (right little finger) and its own key statistic.
- Stars still come only from accuracy; fixing a mistake is praised ("good catch").

## Why
- In real typing (school work, messages) mistakes are not blocked; the child must notice
  and correct them. Today the game simply waits for the right key, so Backspace is never
  practised.
- School typing tools let teachers choose: Backspace on for beginners because the inability to
  correct mistakes could result in frustration, off for advanced typists.

## Fit
- Nothing is lost on a mistake, so "nothing bad happens" still holds.
- Risk: more friction for beginners; keep it off until the letters are learned and offer
  it most in sentence practice.

## Priority
P3, effort S.
