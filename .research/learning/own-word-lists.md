# Own word lists (school spelling words)

## What
- In the parent area, a parent or teacher pastes a list of words (one per line or
  separated by commas), for example this week's spelling or vocabulary words, and gives it
  a name ("Spelling week 12").
- The child sees the list as a "My words" practice on the map: a calm free-practice run that
  uses only these words, and optionally mixes them into story levels.
- Words with keys the child has not learned yet are still allowed in "My words", with the
  guide shown for the new keys; story levels use only words that fit the active keys.
- Several lists per profile; they are part of the profile export, so a teacher can share a
  list file with a class.

## Why
- Parents and teachers want to connect typing with school work. Paid typing
  programs sell a word-list feature where parents paste or type in words in any format to
  build a custom list.
- Programs for dyslexic children let students add their own words from school to practice
  spelling, and parents praise exactly this mix.
- Typing spelling words "allows muscle memory to encode the spelling as a series of key
  strokes".
- Teachers value building custom lessons and assigning them, and users of adaptive typing
  tools ask for their own text.

## Fit
- Fully local, no accounts; fits the parent area and the export file.
- Gives the app a second reason to be opened every week, without streaks.
- Risk: words with unknown keys; solved by guide-on for those keys and by keeping them out
  of story levels. Block list check still applies.

## Priority
P1, effort S. Small change, high value for parents and teachers.
