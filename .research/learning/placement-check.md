# Placement check for children who already type

## What
- When a new profile is made, Tapko asks: "Have you learned some keys already?" If yes, a
  short, calm check (about 2 minutes) shows letters and short words row by row.
- Keys the child types accurately and with the right rhythm are unlocked at once (not marked
  as mastered, so their guide still fades in the normal way); the story still starts at
  world 1, but levels go faster because more keys are open.
- The check can be repeated from the parent area; "No" or skipping starts at `f j`.

## Why
- Children who already know the home row get bored by repeated basics; school typing
  tools have placement tests that unlock skills students are already proficient in.
- A user of a typing site liked the placement test and started at their level right away.
- Lesson pacing that does not match the child can discourage students; it is a top parent
  complaint about typing courses.
- Today the parent can pick keys by hand, but most parents cannot judge which keys their
  child really knows.

## Fit
- Measures accuracy and steady rhythm, not WPM, so it keeps "accuracy first".
- Risk: a child who hunts and pecks fast may pass keys with wrong fingers; unlock only on
  good accuracy and steady reaction time, and keep the guide visible until mastery.

## Priority
P2, effort M.

## Spec
Built as the [placement check](../../.specify/learning/placement-check.md): asked by Ms Turtle on the map, with an honest fingers question first and a timing signal against hunt-and-peck.
