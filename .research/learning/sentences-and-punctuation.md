# Sentences, space bar and punctuation

## What
- A new lesson track after the letters: the **space bar** (thumb), then short **sentences**
  made from known words, with a capital letter at the start and a period at the end.
- New punctuation keys unlock one by one like letters, each with its finger and its own
  intro card: `.` `,` `?` `!` `-` `:` and (Slovak) the right Shift combinations for them.
- In story levels some targets carry a short phrase (2-4 words) instead of one word once the
  track is open; the space bar counts as a key with its own statistics.
- The finger guide shows which thumb presses space and which hand holds Shift (the
  opposite hand to the letter).

## Why
- Today the space bar is never typed and punctuation is never taught, so a child who
  finishes the game still cannot type a normal sentence for school.
- Every structured course ends with sentences and punctuation: Dance Mat Typing goes
  "from individual keys to short words and sentences"
  (https://www.commonsense.org/education/reviews/dance-mat-typing); KeyBlaze and others
  continue "with capitalization, punctuation and numbers"; Monkeytype offers separate
  punctuation and numbers modes (https://www.mintlify.com/monkeytypegame/monkeytype/community/faq).
- A first-grade study counted it as success when children "could type words and short
  sentences without looking at the keyboard"
  (https://vtext.valdosta.edu/xmlui/handle/10428/406).

## Fit
- Same key-by-key unlock and mastery rules; accuracy stays first.
- Risk: long phrases on moving targets can feel hectic; keep phrases short and slow, and
  use calm modes (stepping stones, free practice) for full sentences.
- Slovak needs care: `.` `,` `-` sit on the lower right, `?` `!` `:` need Shift.

## Priority
P1, effort M. Biggest learning gap; everything school-related depends on it.
