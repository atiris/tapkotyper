# Space, punctuation and capitals: when they come in

Refines the order in [sentences-and-punctuation.md](sentences-and-punctuation.md): space, period
and comma move into the letter path instead of a track after it, and capitals come in the
middle of the path, in two steps.

## What

### Proposed order (lesson groups)

Slovak (`sk-qwertz` and `sk-qwerty`, same punctuation keys):

| When (after group) | New key | How it is typed |
|---|---|---|
| with `f j` (group 1, second intro card) | space | thumb |
| `r u` (group 7) | `.` | right ring finger, down from `l` |
| `n` (group 10) | `,` | right middle finger, down from `k` |
| `p` (group 14) | capitals of right-hand letters (J, K, L, M, N, O, P, H, I, U) | left Shift, left pinky |
| `z` (group 16) | capitals of left-hand letters (A, S, D, E, R, T, V, B, C ...) | right Shift, right pinky |
| `q` (group 20, end of base letters) | `?` and `-` | `?` = left Shift + `,` key; `-` = right pinky, down-right, no Shift |
| after the first diacritics (`á í`) | `!` and `:` | `!` = left Shift + `§` key; `:` = left Shift + `.` key |
| diacritics track | capital diacritic letters (Č, Š, Ž ...) only when both the letter and that Shift are known, and rarely | |
| last | digits (unchanged) | |

English (`us-qwerty`):

| When (after group) | New key | How it is typed |
|---|---|---|
| with `f j` | space | thumb |
| `r u` | `.` | as above |
| `n` | `,` and `'` (apostrophe, for `don't`, `it's`) | `'` = right pinky, right of `;` |
| `p` | left Shift (right-hand capitals; `I` is the most common one) | |
| `y` (group 16) | right Shift (left-hand capitals) | |
| `z` (end of letters) | `?` and `:` | `?` = left Shift + `/`; `:` = left Shift + `;` |
| with digits | `!` (Shift + `1`) and `-` | number row, so they come with the digits |

All other symbols (quotes, brackets, `/`, `%`) stay out of the child path; the parent can still
pick them in manual mode.

### How it looks in the game

- **Space.** From the first lesson a target can hold two short chunks with one space, like
  `fj jf`, later two real words, like `les sad`. The space is a key with its own intro card
  (both thumbs, "a thumb hits it, the fingers stay home") and its own statistics. Story targets
  that fly or fall hold at most two words (about 9 letters); calm modes (stepping stones, free
  practice) use 3-4 word phrases once the comma is known.
- **Period.** Ends a short phrase of 2-3 known words: `mama ide.` / `a red hat.` Before
  capitals the phrase starts in lower case. This is fine: TypingClub also types simple
  sentences with periods and commas before capitals.
- **Comma.** Inside 3-4 word phrases: lists and simple pauses, `ema, eva a jano.` (calm modes),
  short pairs in story targets.
- **Capitals.** Only at the start of a target: a name (`Jano`, `Ema`, `Tom`) or the first word of
  a phrase. Never two capitals in one word, no CAPS words. Rate grows with the Shift key's
  readiness: about 1 target in 6 at unlock, up to "every phrase starts with a capital" when it
  is mastered. While a Shift step is new, the time limit for a capital letter is longer.
  The finger guide lights the correct Shift (the other hand); if the child uses the wrong one,
  the letter counts, but Tapko shows a short tip ("the other pinky holds Shift").
- **`?` and `!`.** End of short question or call phrases: `kde je mama?`, `Hura!`.

## Why

- **Space early is normal.** Typing.com's first key lesson is "J, F, and Space"
  (https://www.typing.com/es/curriculum/keyboarding). Dance Mat Typing assigns the space bar to
  the thumbs in stage 1, with the home row
  (https://www.kidztype.com/blog/category/bbc-mat-typing-stage-1/). Today tapkotyper never
  types a space, so the thumb is never trained and words never join into text.
- **Period and comma come with the letters, not after them.** Typing.com: "Period and Comma"
  is lesson 12 of 16 letter lessons, after V, H, M and before W, X, Q, Y, P, Z
  (https://www.typing.com/es/curriculum/keyboarding). Dance Mat Typing: comma with C in stage 9,
  period in stage 11 of 12 (https://www.kidztype.com/blog/?p=302). TypingClub (Typing Jungle)
  teaches `, .` with the bottom row (lessons 52-88), then "Basic Level 1": "simple sentences
  using the whole alphabet, commas, and periods ... before introducing capital letters"
  (https://login.typingclub.com/m/edclubdocs/media/pdf/typing-jungle-handbook.pdf). Our order
  pulls them a bit earlier because they are close to the home row (`.` and `,` are one step
  down from `l` and `k`) and because a period makes even 2-word targets feel like real text.
- **Capitals: big courses put them after all letters; that is the "end block" to avoid.**
  Typing.com: "Shift Key and Capitalization" opens part 2, after all letters. TypingClub:
  Shift after letters and basic sentences (lessons 192-202), then "Basic Level 2": "full
  sentences using capitals and simple punctuation ... practice using the shift key in context"
  (same handbook). Dance Mat: Shift is the last stage. Ratatype: "Using the Shift Key" is
  lesson 10 of 12 (https://www.ratatype.com/faq/ratatype-curriculum/). At the other end,
  typing-lessons.org teaches Shift as lesson 5, right after the home row
  (https://www.typing-lessons.org/lesson_5.html). Our middle point (after the home and top rows
  are known) gives enough real words for names and sentence starts, and in Slovak it puts
  capitals before the long diacritics track, so they do not become the last block.
- **Opposite-hand Shift** is the standard rule: "pressed by the pinky finger on the hand
  opposite to the one hitting the key" (https://www.typing-lessons.org/lesson_5.html);
  TypingClub uses it so "students maintain the correct finger placement and easily return to
  the home row" (Typing Jungle handbook). Splitting it into left and right Shift halves the new
  motor pattern at each step, like the mirrored pairs we already use.
- **Shift chords are hard for children.** A study with children with special needs found
  "shift and letter ... is difficult, not only the physical movement (pressing two keys at a
  time) but also remembering these combinations"
  (https://edusoft.fmi.uni-sofia.bg/specialneeds/PAGE4_Files/PAPER5_EN.pdf). A keystroke study
  of Japanese primary pupils found errors with punctuation and Shift operation 12 times more
  frequent than other keyboard operations, at every grade
  (https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/browse/jmet/14/0/_contents/-char/en). Keyboarding needs
  bilateral coordination and isolated finger use, like handwriting
  (https://geneseocsd.org/keyboarding). Hence: low starting rate, only at word start, longer
  time limit, one Shift side at a time.
- **One key at a time, also for punctuation.** Keybr adds about ten punctuation keys at once
  when the option is on, some needing Shift, and users ask for them one by one like letters
  (https://alternativeto.net/software/keybr/about,
  https://groups.google.com/g/keybr/c/5DcHT3oySXU).
- **Slovak school method (ZAV)** goes from letters to words, word groups and sentences in
  small steps and asks for "writing correct capital letters" as an outcome
  (https://archiv.mpc-edu.sk/sites/default/files/projekty/vystup/9_ops_sotakova_tatiana_-_metodika_pisania_na_pocitaci_desatprstovou_hmatovou_metodou_v_programe_zav.pdf);
  no public source gave its exact key order.

## Fit

- **Lesson groups.** New entries in `SK_GROUPS` / `US_GROUPS`: `' '` in group 1, `.` `,` `?` `-`
  `!` `:` (`'` for US) as single-key groups at the places above, and the single `Shift` pseudo-key
  split into two (left Shift, right Shift), each measured on the capitals it serves. Same
  ready / mastery rules; space and Shift steps should not block the next letter for long.
- **Words.** Today words are single words. Needed: a small phrase builder (2-4 known words,
  optional comma, period or `?` at the end, optional capital at the start, plus a short list of
  child names per language). The pseudo-word fallback joins chunks with spaces but adds no
  punctuation inside chunks. "Words without diacritics" keeps working.
- **Modes.** Story targets: max 2 words, so falling targets stay short. Calm modes get the longer
  phrases. Manual key mode: the parent can switch space, punctuation and capitals on and off.
- **Stats.** Space and each punctuation key get key stats; capitals feed the Shift side stat
  and the letter stat. The existing single `Shift` stat is replaced (no backward compatibility
  needed before production).
- **Risks.** Space from lesson 1 makes the very first targets a bit longer; keep group 1 targets
  to two 2-letter chunks. Lower-case sentences with a period may look wrong to parents for a few
  lessons; the intro card can say "capitals come soon". Wrong-Shift detection needs
  `KeyboardEvent.code` (already used for layout checks). Capital diacritics in Slovak need dead
  key + Shift + letter, which is a three-step chord; keep them rare.

## Priority

P1, effort M. Space, period and comma first (they unlock real text and are cheap); the
left/right Shift split second.
