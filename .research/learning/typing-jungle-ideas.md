# Ideas from the Typing Jungle course (edclub / TypingClub)

Date: 2026-11-04. Source: https://www.edclub.com/student/course/3 (685 lessons), opened as a guest, and the
course data the page loads (each lesson's type, game, text and settings). Numbers below are the lesson
numbers shown on that page, so each one can be opened there. The "Play" games from lesson 23 on partly
need the paid plan.

## Worth taking over

### 1. One hand types, the other holds its anchor key (P1)
- **Seen in:** 17, 18, 32, 33, 48, 49, 79, 80, 93, 99, 105, 111, 117, 123 (letters), 267, 268, 271, 272 (numbers).
  The left-hand lessons say "Hold the J key while typing this lesson", the right-hand ones hold F.
- **Why:** the resting index finger cannot drift, so the hand learns that home is a place you come back to.
  The typing hand also cannot get help from the other one.
- **For Tapko:** an early level step once the child knows at least four keys of each hand: one hand types
  words of its own keys, the other holds F or J. When the held key is let go, the scene waits calmly
  ("Put your finger back on J") and goes on once it is pressed again. Nothing is lost. Later the same
  step works on the number row (1-5 while J is held, 6-0 while F is held).

### 2. Finger column trips (P2)
- **Seen in:** 55, 56, 60, 61, 67, 68, 72, 73, 84, 85 (letters, for example `mju` and `vfr`),
  261-266, 269, 270, 273 (numbers), 333-344 (symbols).
- **Why:** one finger goes up and down its own column and back home. This trains the reach, not new keys.
- **For Tapko:** a short calm step per finger after its column keys are known, for example `jum`,
  `fvr`, `ki,` and on the Slovak number row `ľ`, `š`, `č`.

### 3. Tricky words: a sentence, then the word five times (P1 for Slovak)
- **Seen in:** 127-136, 234-243, 419-428 (for example `Accept/Except`, `Desert/Dessert`).
- **Why:** spelling and typing together. The word comes in a sentence first, then repeated.
- **For Tapko:** it fits Slovak school spelling very well: the *vybrané slová* (`byť/biť`, `mi/my`,
  `výr/vír`), and `ä`, `ô`, `ĺ`, `ŕ`. It could be a word set for the parent's own words or a world's step.

### 4. Common letter groups (P2)
- **Seen in:** 192-201, 275-284, 347-356 (`the`, `ing`, `tion`, `ound` ...).
- **Why:** frequent groups become one movement.
- **For Tapko:** Slovak groups like `nie`, `ova`, `pre`, `sta`, `ný`, `ch`, `ostí`, as a step after
  the needed keys are known.

### 5. Texts that teach while they are typed (P3)
- **Seen in:** 92 *Don't Look Down*, 107 *Brain Power*, 120 *Bumps on J & F*, 616 *Left Hand Typing*,
  640 *Tongue Twisters*.
- **Why:** the typed text is itself a typing tip, so the tip costs no extra time.
- **For Tapko:** some of the preparation tips could also come as a typed text in a story page or task.

### 6. Rhyming words as a game's word set (P3)
- **Seen in:** 470, 475.
- **For Tapko:** a word set of rhymes (`mak, rak, pták`). It is light fun with the same keys.

## Already in Tapko, or not wanted

- New key introduced alone, a mistake must be typed right: 2, 3, 5, 9 ... (Tapko has key cards).
- A sentence that names the reach, "move your right index finger one key to the left": 22 (Tapko's
  key card shows the finger).
- Adaptive practice of weak keys at each part's end: 88, 126, 137, 191, 202, 233 ... (Tapko already
  weights weak keys).
- Hands over the keyboard that light up the finger: 107 (Tapko has them).
- Fact texts to type: 203-232 (see [../game/story-pages.md](../game/story-pages.md)).
- Short videos: 1, 16, 30, 46, 63, 78, 86, 125, 138 (no, see the rule: no intro popups before play).
- Games: *BalloonValley* 8, 15 (a ninja jumps over balloons with letters, three hearts), *MonsterAttack*
  34, 94 (a word in tiles, each letter throws a thing at a monster), *TempleBash* 23, *FloatingBubbles*
  106, 124, *AppleThieves* 300, 315. Their ideas are close to Tapko's levels. Lives (hearts) are not
  wanted (accuracy-first, no game over).
