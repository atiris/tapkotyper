# Gentle weekly goal

## What
- The parent (or the child together with the parent) picks a small weekly goal, for
  example "3 short sessions" or "30 minutes this week".
- On the map a little plant (or the dragon egg nest) grows a bit after each session. When
  the goal is met, Tapko celebrates once and the plant blooms.
- Missing the goal changes nothing: no wilting, no "you lost your streak", no reminder
  messages. A new week simply starts a new plant; old blooms stay in a small garden.
- The dashboard shows weeks with the goal met, next to time per day.

## Why
- Parents want regular practice; a Typing.com user, otherwise happy, "wished for a daily
  goal setting" (https://de.trustpilot.com/review/typing.com?page=6), and Typing.com now
  sells daily goals of "5 to 60 minutes a day" with a celebration when met
  (https://support.typing.com/en/articles/9051073).
- Slovak teaching guides say training takes "about 3 months of regular practice, at least
  10 minutes a day" (https://zssambra.sk/blog/nacvik-pisania-na-pc-doc-ucebna-pomocka/), so
  regularity matters more than long sessions.
- A weekly (not daily) goal that never punishes avoids the streak pressure the app
  rejects on purpose.

## Fit
- Must respect principle 3 (no streaks, no "come back or lose"): progress only grows, never
  shrinks, and there are no notifications (the app has none anyway).
- Risk: even a soft goal can feel like homework; off by default, parent turns it on.
