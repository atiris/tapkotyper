# Easter eggs

Small secret surprises for a child who types somewhere outside a level. They reward
curiosity and typing itself, never grant anything and never get in the way: nothing is
saved, a click ends them, and they do not appear behind a dialog or in a text
field. Every secret word is the first word of a hint the page shows (or of a title on it), so
the words follow the interface language without a list of their own; capitals and diacritics
do not matter ("Dračie", "dračie" and "dracie" are one word), and a short word (such as "hi")
counts only on its own, not inside another word. Not mentioned in the help: finding them is the fun.

- **Start page: a greeting.** The fox head of the logo says on hover "Ahoj, ja som Ťapko." /
  "Hi, I'm Tapko.". Typing its first word ("ahoj" / "hi") brings Tapko of a random kind in a
  random outfit (any item of the wardrobe, like its random button), as tall as the grass,
  walking in from the left. It hops, says hello, says it is Tapko and invites the child to pick
  a player and train for a while, then walks away after a few seconds or on a click.
- **Map: the target range.** Typing the first word of the range's hint ("strelnica" /
  "target") sends a random thing of the target range drifting across the page from left to
  right at an easy pace, turning slowly, with a whoosh. With reduced motion it does not come.
- **Map: the parrot.** Typing the first word of the parrot's hint ("papagáj" / "parrot") calls a parrot that
  flies in from one side, flutters through a few random places over the page beating its
  wings (three pictures, wings up, middle and down, played there and back, the body lifting a
  little on each downstroke), always facing where it flies, squawks and leaves on the other side. With reduced
  motion it does not come.
- **My progress: the badge show.** Typing one of the longer words of the story titles or of
  the badges' heading ("Dračie", "Robot", "Víla", "odznaky", ... / "Dragon", "Robot",
  "Fairy", "badges", ...) darkens the screen: a random world's badge in a random tier rises
  in rotating golden rays and a pulsing glow with twinkling sparkles, a random range award
  lands on its rim, and the caption names both. It is a show only, the child's real badges do
  not change. It has no close button: it fades after ten seconds, on a click or on Esc.
