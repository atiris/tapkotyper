# Easter eggs

Small secret surprises for a child who types somewhere outside a level. They reward
curiosity and typing itself, never grant anything and never get in the way: nothing is
saved, a click ends them, and they do not appear behind a dialog or in a text
field. The words follow the interface language, capitals and diacritics do not matter
("Dračie", "dračie" and "dracie" are one word), and a short word (such as "hi") counts only
on its own, not inside another word. Not mentioned in the help: finding them is the fun.

- **Start page: a greeting.** Typing a greeting ("ahoj", "čau", "dobrý deň", ... /
  "hi", "hello", "good morning", ...) brings Tapko of a random kind in a random outfit (any
  item of the wardrobe, like its random button), as tall as the grass, walking in from the
  left. It hops, says hello, says it is Tapko and invites the child to pick a player and train for a while,
  then walks away after a few seconds or on a click. A space inside a greeting does not press
  the focused player card.
- **Map: the parrot.** Typing the parrot's name ("papagáj" / "parrot") calls a parrot that
  flies in from one side, flutters through a few random places over the page beating its
  wings (three pictures, wings up, middle and down, played there and back, the body lifting a
  little on each downstroke), always facing where it flies, squawks and leaves on the other side. With reduced
  motion it does not come.
- **My progress: the badge show.** Typing one of the longer words of the story titles or of
  the badges' heading ("Dračie", "Robot", "Víla", "odznaky", ... / "Dragon", "Robot",
  "Fairy", "badges", ...) darkens the screen: a random world's badge in a random tier rises
  in rotating golden rays and a pulsing glow with twinkling sparkles, a random range award
  lands on its rim, and the caption names both. It is a show only, the child's real badges do
  not change. It has no close button: it fades after ten seconds, on a click or on Esc.
