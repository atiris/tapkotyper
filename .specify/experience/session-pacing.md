# Session pacing

## What
- Sessions are short: a soft end after the parent-set length (default 15 minutes). Tapko
  suggests a stretch break with a short animated exercise; the child may finish the
  current level first.
- No daily streaks, no "come back or lose" messages, no random loot.
- Intros and cutscenes are always skippable.

- Tapko's lines stay at least 3.5 seconds (0.08 s per character), are not replaced while shown,
  and wait while a target would be covered.

## Why
- 5-15 minute sessions with breaks keep focus high and fatigue low; streaks and variable rewards
  create unhealthy pressure ([research](../../.research/features/session-pacing.md)).
