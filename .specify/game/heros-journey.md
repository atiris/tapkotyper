# Hero's journey

## What
- **Cesta hrdinu** / **Hero's journey**: one long, harder trip that closes each story. Its
  symbol is the **compass**, the picture of its map node.
- It sits in the card of the story's last world, right after the crown of its super level
  and before the target range and the parrot. It opens with that world done, the same moment
  as the range and the parrot; before that the node is there, faded, with the tooltip "opens
  when the world is finished".
- It plays one part for every world of the story, in world order. Each part is the game of
  that world's first level, on that world's background. A story may define its own journey
  instead. A world not built yet plays its placeholder ([story](story.md)).
- Only the get-ready card comes before the first part. Between parts a short travel card names
  the next world ("Ďalej: <world>"), with the world's picture as on the map; Space, Enter or a
  click goes on. No other intro cards and no key cards.
- It lasts twice the minimum of a regular level of its story, split evenly over the parts; the
  planned words likewise ([mechanics](mechanics.md)).
- The play is harder, and the get-ready card says so in one line: the difficulty is one step
  above the child's (at most 10), the words are harder (a letter longer, and longer phrases),
  and three stars need 98 % accuracy.
- Stars come from the accuracy of the whole journey, one step stricter than a level: 3 stars
  from 98 %, 2 from 95 %, else 1. The card shows halves: 3 from 98 %, 2.5 from 96.5 %, 2 from
  95 %, 1.5 from 90 %, else 1. The paw rule (fewer than 5 own keys gives one star) holds over
  the whole journey.
- It counts like a level: its keys, time and rhythm go into the [statistics](../profiles/statistics.md),
  and growth and the lesson advance as after a story level. The best stars show as mini stars
  on its node and add to the star total (234).
- It is not a level of its world: it gives no badge, does not finish the world and opens
  nothing. The parent's surprises count it as a level of the story's last world
  ([parent controls](../profiles/parent-controls.md)).
- The end card (the one card of every end, see [mechanics](mechanics.md)) is "Cesta hrdinu
  prejdená!" with the compass, the stars, accuracy, rhythm and minutes. Buttons: "Hrať znova" and
  the map. Staying a while is not offered.
- Leaving midway (pause card, "Ukončiť písanie") asks first, like a story level; the progress
  of the journey is lost.

## Why
- A second, weaker reward loop beside the story is not needed. What is worth having is one
  final test that closes a story.
- A long, harder trip through all the worlds of the story lets the child show how much they
  learned; counting like a real level makes it matter for stars and growth.
- Stricter stars give a goal for children who finished the story with ease, and the get-ready
  card tells the difficulty up front, so it is never a surprise.
- Breaking the length into one part per world keeps it varied and short enough in each part;
  the travel card gives a moment to breathe.
- It does not open anything and is not a level of its world, so a child never needs it to move
  on in the story.
