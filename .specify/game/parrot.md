# Parrot

## What
- **Papagáj** / **Parrot**: a voluntary side card in every world, next to the target range. The
  child types the same few words many times over, so the fingers learn them (muscle memory).
  Speed is not judged and there are no stars; slow typing is fine, the point is the practice.
- It opens with the world's super level, like the [target range](target-practice.md): a small
  parrot node after the range's target on the map. The node is grey while the world's parrot is
  not won (or after it flew away) and in full colour while it is at home; its tooltip says what
  it trains. Before the world is finished the node is already there, grey and closed like a
  level not reached yet (the tooltip adds when it opens). It starts only when the child picks
  it, never by "Continue".
- **Scene:** the world's own background. Tapko stands at the left, the parrot sits on a perch at
  the right. The word to type is Tapko's bubble between them; the next word waits greyed after
  it. When a word is typed, the parrot repeats it in its own bubble with a short squawk.
- **Round:** first the words in turn, three rounds (dom strom časy dom strom časy dom strom
  časy), then each word several times in a row; Tapko says when the second part starts. A wrong
  key brings the current word twice more, at most six more per word, so a round always ends.
- **Size** by the world's story and its place in it (worlds 1-2 of a story shorter words):

  | | Dragon story | Robot story | Fairy story |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Words | 3 | 4 | 5 |
  | Letters per word | 2-4, then 3-5 | 4-5, then 4-6 | 5-6, then 5-7 |
  | Repeats per word in part 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |

  The words use the letters of the world's last level: real words where the letters allow it
  (letter drills in the meadow's tiny set), all different and starting with different letters
  where possible, new ones each round.
- The typing counts in the statistics and in the lesson like at the target range; paws, Enter
  marks and the parent's surprises stay out.
- **End card:** the parrot in colour, "Papagáj sa naučil tvoje slová!", the words learned, how
  fast the fingers were (the child's usual speed of the last 14 days, the learning part and the
  repeating part, the last one green when it was faster), new letters when the lesson moved on,
  and the Parrot badge with its line when this win brought it. "Nové slová" plays again with new
  words; the X at the top right and Esc go to the map. Finishing the round wins the world's
  parrot.
- **Parrots fly away**, so the child comes back and practises again:
  - one roll for each day since the last check (only today the first time): a 20 % chance that
    one random parrot at home flies away; a child back after 13 days gets 13 rolls;
  - never below the floor: the most parrots ever at home minus 3, and at least 1 (the last one
    stays);
  - 21 days after the last win the parrots drop straight to the floor.
  The roll runs when a player's map opens. The map then says it once in the narrator's bubble
  ("Uletel ti papagáj zo sveta Lúka. Choď poň!", several worlds in one line), and each parrot
  flies up and out of the window from its node in about two seconds while the node turns grey.
  With less motion the nodes simply turn grey.
- **Parrot badge** (Odznak Papagáj): held while the parrot of every finished world is at home
  (at least one). A newly finished world without its parrot takes it away until that parrot is
  won, and so does a parrot flying away (the badge next to the name shrinks and fades with the
  flight). It is worth 7 points in the treasure chest ([statistics](../profiles/statistics.md))
  and it is the one badge shown with the player: on the start page's player card as the parrot
  itself at the character's feet, by its left side; the badge picture on the map's greeting
  and just under the character on "Môj postup".

## Why
- Word streams and the range train reading and reacting; a few words typed many times train the
  movement itself, which is what makes typing blind.
- Parrots flying away bring the child back to old worlds for short practice, without any
  penalty: "a parrot flew away, go get it back". The floor and the last parrot keep it from
  feeling like losing everything.
- One badge next to the name gives a visible, regainable goal that rewards regular practice.
