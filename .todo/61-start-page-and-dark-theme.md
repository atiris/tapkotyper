# 61 - Start page redesign and a dark theme everywhere

## Why
The start page is the first thing a visitor sees and it is too plain: it does not say what
TapkoTyper is, has no logo on small screens, the TapkoTyper title does not match the logo's
style, and the meadow strip under it was a cut piece of a level layer. The owner also wants a
dark theme ("dark enough, not black") that works in every part of the game, switched on the
start page with a short animated change from day to night and back.

Reference design (canvas with every size, player count and both themes; read it with the
Artifact tool, it is the owner's approved direction):
https://claude.ai/artifact/PBKizYqqib9bonGxstiQtz
The pictures it uses are already in `public/assets/` (commit 4dbde0e): `ui-start-meadow`,
`ui-start-meadow-night`, `ui-start-sun`, `ui-start-moon`, `ui-start-star`, `ui-start-cloud-1`,
`ui-start-cloud-2`, plus `obj-butterfly`, `obj-butterfly-2`, `obj-butterfly-3`, `obj-firefly`.
Where the canvas and this plan differ, this plan wins (it holds the owner's later decisions:
sky pieces on the left, the day/night arc, the softer wordmark, the two-line slogan, the
language button).
No picture is generated or regenerated by this plan.

## Decisions (taken; nobody to ask)

### Theme
- Default `'system'` follows `prefers-color-scheme` live (matchMedia listener). The first click
  on the theme button stores the opposite of what is shown (`'light'` or `'dark'`); later clicks
  toggle. The resolved theme is `data-theme="light|dark"` on `<html>` before first paint
  (`src/main.ts` reads the store synchronously or a tiny inline script in `index.html` reads a
  mirrored `localStorage` key `tt-theme`; pick the one that gives no light flash on a dark reload).
- Colors are tokens on `:root`, a dark set under `:root[data-theme='dark']`. Dark palette (from
  the design): page sky `#171631 -> #23213e -> #1e2830`, paper `#2b2742`, paper-2 `#342f4e`,
  ink `#f4ecdf`, ink-soft `#c2b7d2`, line `rgba(255,240,220,.16)`, round button disc stays the
  meaning color, the white circle becomes `#3a3456`. Brand orange and the meaning colors stay;
  shades used as text on dark get lighter variants that pass WCAG AA.
- High contrast mode keeps its own black-on-white scheme and wins over the theme.
- Stays light in dark mode: the certificate (it is printed), the level background art and all
  sprites (they are pictures; they have transparent backgrounds and dark outlines, which work on
  dark). The level's HUD, cards, dialogs and bars follow the theme.
- Charts and canvases read the tokens (`getComputedStyle`) when they draw, and redraw on a theme
  change.

### Theme button
- A round button in the global corner controls (purple, the controls color), between the
  language and help, on every screen with the standard placement; in the Menu panel below 600 px
  with its label. Not in a level's compact play bar. Its symbol is a moon in light and a sun in
  dark; tooltip/label "Tmavý režim" / "Svetlý režim" ("Dark mode" / "Light mode").

### Languages: room for more than two
- The language list is data, not code branches: `src/i18n/i18n.ts` keeps one table of the
  supported languages with each one's native name (`sk` "Slovenčina", `en` "English") and a
  table of close languages (`cs` -> `sk`, the mapping `detectLanguage` already has). Adding a
  language = its text file, one table row, its word list and layout (localization.md). `Lang`
  in `src/storage/schema.ts` follows the table; no storage change.
- Default when nothing is stored: walk `navigator.languages` in order and take the first one
  whose primary subtag is supported, else whose close language is; else English. A stored
  choice wins.
- The SK/EN chips and the round button showing SK or EN go away. One round button (purple,
  controls) with the international language symbol (the glyph pair "文A" drawn as inline SVG in
  the round button style; aria-label and tooltip "Jazyk: Slovenčina" / "Language: English")
  opens a small list of the languages by native name, the current one marked. Arrow keys move,
  Enter picks, Escape or a click outside closes; focus returns to the button. Same button at
  every width; in the Menu panel below 600 px it is an item that opens the same list.
- The new player form's language choice and the parent settings use the same table (a select
  or the same list), so a third language shows up everywhere at once.

### Start page layout (see the canvas)
- Two columns from 900 px width: left the title block, right the card "Kto sa dnes hrá?".
  Below 900 px one column in this order: title, slogan, the card, then the text and the bullets
  (the card wins on small screens).
- Title block: the wordmark TapkoTyper as live text in the logo's style: layered text, a
  dark-brown outline `#3a2216` of `.042em` with a `.01em .022em` shadow, fills as gradients from
  light at the top left to dark at the bottom right (Tapko orange `#ffe1c2 -> #ffab66 -> #f47b2a
  -> #d65a12`, Typer green `#e2f7d4 -> #97dba6 -> #4fae6e -> #2f8a52`); in dark a thin cream line
  outside the outline (`.085em` stroke, `rgba(255,240,222,.85)`), never a glow. No logo beside
  the title.
- Slogan always on exactly two lines: "Desať prstov, / jedno veľké dobrodružstvo." ("Ten fingers,
  / one big adventure."), each line `nowrap`, font size fitted to the column (the longer line is
  about 14 em; caps 44 px normal, 32 px short screens, 58 px from 1000 px height, 40 px one column).
- Text: "Tapko ťa prevedie pokojnými svetmi plnými príbehov. S každou úrovňou pribudne pár
  kláves, až kým nepíšeš všetkými desiatimi bez pozerania." Bullets with green stroke icons:
  "Bez reklám a bez účtu", "Údaje ostávajú v zariadení" (a link to the help's about tab, the
  old footer's job), "Šatník a hviezdičky za snahu", "Pokrok vidia rodičia aj mentori"; English
  equivalents. Bullets go to one column when the left column is under 540 px.
- The card: the logo face (`ui-icon-hr`) sits half above its top edge, centered; no character.
  No players: the line "Stačí meno a vybrať si zvieratko.", the big orange "Nový hráč", then
  "Skúsiť bez profilu" and "Načítať profily". With players: the player cards in a row, plus the
  dashed "Nový hráč" card while there are 1-3 players; from 4 players "Nový hráč" becomes a button
  beside "Bez profilu" and "Načítať profily". The card grows with its cards; 6+ players wrap in
  balanced rows (existing `rowSplit`). Number keys keep picking players.
- No footer. Bottom right on the meadow: "MIT · Atiris" (link to the GitHub repo), 12 px,
  white with a soft shadow, about 70 % opacity. Hover and focus keep it white (full opacity,
  underline, the focus ring), in both themes: the global link and hover colors must not reach it
  (the canvas turned it dark on hover, unreadable on the grass). The visit counter leaves the start page (stays
  in help).
- Copy follows the terms rule in localization.md: no "dieťa/deti/žiak/učiteľ" or "child/kid/
  teacher" (the canvas's phone text says "deti"; do not copy that). Phone text: "Pokojná hra, v
  ktorej sa s Tapkom naučíš písať všetkými desiatimi. Bez reklám, bez účtu, údaje ostávajú v
  zariadení." The keyboard button keeps its current texts (`start.gate.*`).
- Phone without a keyboard (gated): logo face above the wordmark, slogan, a short text, then the
  mentors' button as the main orange one directly under the text, the keyboard button below it
  as the secondary (paper with a border).
- 1366 x 768 and 1024 x 600 never scroll with up to 5 players (existing rule in visuals.md).
  Content keeps clear of the meadow's dense grass; flower tips may pass behind the card.

### The sky and the meadow (`src/ui/sky.ts`)
- Meadow: `ui-start-meadow` and `ui-start-meadow-night` as two stacked layers repeated along the
  bottom (`background-repeat: repeat-x`, both with the same `background-size` computed from the
  day tile, so they line up). Heights: 190 px normal, 140 px under 700 px height, 260 px from
  1000 px, 200 px one column, 130 px phone.
- Sky pieces stay in the top left part of the page (left of the card and the logo face, above
  the title block), never behind text: sun or moon, two clouds by day, three stars by night. On
  a short screen the pieces shrink, and a piece that would touch text is left out.
- Depth blur (farther is softer): meadow 0.6 px, clouds 2 px, sun 4 px, moon 2.5 px, stars 1 px.
- Idle motion: clouds drift slowly side to side, stars twinkle, 3 butterflies (one of each
  sprite: `obj-butterfly` blue-yellow, `obj-butterfly-2` coral, `obj-butterfly-3` peacock)
  flutter in small loops low over the meadow by day, 3-4 fireflies drift and glow low over the
  meadow by night. Nothing crosses text. Fireflies use `obj-firefly` small (24-36 px) with a
  soft warm CSS glow; if its pink keying edge shows at that size, draw them as glowing CSS dots
  instead (no new picture).
- The change (about 2.5 s, like a sped-up night and day): to day, the moon runs to the right
  along an elliptical arc and sets, the sun rises from the left along the same arc to its place,
  the stars fade out, the fireflies sink into the grass and go out, then the butterflies fly up
  out of the grass; the sky and the meadow cross-fade. To night the same in reverse: the sun runs
  right and sets, the moon rises from the left, the butterflies settle into the grass, the
  fireflies rise out of it, the stars fade in. An arc is a rotating wrapper around a pivot below
  the sky band, scaled vertically into an ellipse (or `offset-path`).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` and the game's reduce motion setting: no travel, a 0.3 s cross-fade,
  no idle motion.
- Only the start page has this scene; other screens just take the theme's page background.
